# Booking

## What are the booking statuses?

- **PENDING** — you submitted the request and ServeGo24 is waiting for a provider to accept
- **CONFIRMED** — a provider accepted the job
- **ONGOING** — the provider has arrived and the work is in progress
- **COMPLETED** — the work is finished and the quotation was confirmed
- **CANCELLED** — the booking was cancelled

A booking normally moves PENDING → CONFIRMED → ONGOING → COMPLETED, and it can be cancelled
from PENDING, CONFIRMED or ONGOING. COMPLETED and CANCELLED are final. You are notified at
every status change.

## What happens after I submit a booking?

1. The booking is created as **Pending** and you get a booking number.
2. The request goes to every eligible provider who offers that service.
3. The first provider to accept confirms your booking; the other requests close.
4. The provider marks **On the Way**, then **Arrived**, and does the work.
5. The provider sends you a **quotation**.
6. You confirm the quotation and the booking is completed.

If nobody accepts, the request is sent out again to all eligible providers after 24 hours
rather than being closed, so it stays open until a provider takes it.

## How does a provider get chosen?

Eligible providers are not ranked. Everyone eligible gets the request at the same time and
the first acceptance wins. Distance, rating and provider level do not affect who is offered
the job.

## How do I track the provider?

Once a provider accepts, the booking shows their live location on a map. They mark **On the
Way** when they leave, and are marked **Arrived** automatically when their location comes
within about **150 metres** of your address. If GPS is unavailable they can mark arrival
themselves.

## How does the quotation work?

After the work, the provider sends a **quotation** — a list of their own payable line items.
The quotation total is the booking amount.

- A quotation contains only the provider's line items. There is no service-fee row in it.
- It can be sent while the booking is **Confirmed**, and stays open for **24 hours**.
- You either **confirm** it — which completes the booking — or **decline** it.
- Declining after seeing the quotation is settled differently from an ordinary cancellation.
  See the Cancellation & Support section.

## Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, while it is Pending, Confirmed or Ongoing. Who can cancel:

- The **customer** can cancel their own booking
- The **provider** can cancel a job they have taken
- The **admin** can cancel when resolving a dispute

Cancelling before a quotation was sent has no fee. Declining a quotation you have already
seen does — see the Cancellation & Support section.

## Who can move a booking to the next status?

- The **provider** accepts the job, marks arrival, and marks the work started and completed
- The **customer** cancels
- The **admin** can override a booking to Confirmed when assigning a provider manually, and
  can resolve disputes

Every status change is checked against the booking's current status, so a repeated or stale
request cannot move a booking twice.
