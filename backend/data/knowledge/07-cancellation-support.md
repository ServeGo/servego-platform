# Cancellation & Support

## What happens if I cancel a booking?

It depends on whether you have already seen a quotation.

**Cancel before a quotation was sent** — the booking becomes **Cancelled** and the provider
is notified. There is no fee.

**Decline a quotation you have already seen** — this is settled differently:

- A fixed **cancellation fee** applies. Its default is **₹249**, and the admin can configure
  it.
- The platform **commission** on that fee (10% by default) is debited from the **provider's
  wallet**, even though you cancelled.
- The provider **keeps the fee** as compensation for the trip, recorded as lifetime earnings.
- Your account shows a **display-only** "Cancellation Fee" record. It is not a real charge —
  no money is taken from you, and it never affects your wallet.

Settlement is ledger-only. Real money changes hands directly between you and the provider;
ServeGo24 records wallet state and does not move the money.

## Why is there a fee if I cancel after the provider has travelled?

Because the provider has already spent time and travel cost getting to you. The fee is their
compensation for that trip, not a platform charge.

## Can a provider cancel my booking?

Yes. A provider can decline a request before accepting it, and can also cancel a job they
have already accepted. A provider cancelling after accepting is recorded against their
cancellation record.

## What if my provider has not arrived?

Open the booking's live tracking map. It shows the provider's real-time location and whether
they have marked **On the Way** or **Arrived**. Providers are marked **Arrived**
automatically when they come within about 150 metres of your address.

If the provider is significantly late or unresponsive, raise a **support ticket** and link
the booking. The admin team can see the full booking timeline and resolve the dispute.

## How do I raise a complaint?

Submit a **support ticket**:

- From your dashboard if you are signed in — you can link the related booking
- From the public contact form on the website if you are not signed in — name, email, subject
  and message are enough

The admin team reviews tickets and responds with a resolution.

## Who do I contact for urgent help?

Raise a support ticket. It is the official channel, it is tracked, and it is the only way to
reach a human at ServeGo24. The admin team can review a booking timeline, resolve disputes
between a customer and a provider, and take corrective action.

## Can I cancel a permanent or contract request?

Yes. You can cancel a permanent, custom or no-provider request yourself, and the status
change is reflected for the admin team and any assigned provider.
