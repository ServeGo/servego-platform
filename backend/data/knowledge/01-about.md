# About ServeGo24

## What is ServeGo24?
ServeGo24 is a home services marketplace that connects customers with trusted local service providers for services such as AC repair, plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, and more.
Customers can book a service through the website, and ServeGo24 connects the request with an eligible nearby provider. The provider visits the customer, completes the service, and provides a quotation for the work.
In simple terms, ServeGo24 makes it easy for customers to find and book local service providers for their home service needs.

## Who is on the platform?

There are three roles:

- **Customer** — Requests and books home services through the ServeGo24 platform.
- **Provider** — A verified professional who accepts service requests and visits the customer's location to complete the required work.
- **Admin** — The ServeGo24 operations team responsible for managing providers, services, bookings, and overall platform operations.

You sign up as either a Customer or a Provider. Admin accounts are created by ServeGo24 and
are not open for sign-up.

## How does a booking work end to end?

1. Customer Sign-Up — The customer creates an account using their phone number or email address, provides their home address, and pins their location on the map.
2. The customer picks a service and submits a booking request.
3. The customer does **not** pick a specific provider.
4. ServeGo24 sends the job to every eligible provider who offers that service.
5. The first provider to accept gets the job; everyone else's request is closed.
6. The provider travels to the customer, does the work and sends a **quotation**.
7. The customer reviews the quotation and confirms it, which completes the booking.
8. The customer pays the provider directly. ServeGo24 records the payment and the platform
   commission in its ledger.

## Which providers receive my job request?

A provider receives your request only when **all** of these are true:

- They offer the service you booked
- The ServeGo24 admin has verified their profile
- Their account is active
- Their wallet balance is not negative

Note: being offline, having an incomplete profile, or being outside your area does **not**
by itself stop a provider from receiving your request.

## How are providers chosen once several receive the request?
Service requests are not ranked or prioritized based on provider level, rating, or distance.
The request is sent simultaneously to all eligible providers who offer that service. The first eligible provider to accept the request receives the job, while the request is automatically closed for other providers.

## What are the ServeGo24 IDs?

- **Booking number** — for example `SG24-0001`, shown when a booking is created
- **Customer ID** — for example `CID-0001`
- **Provider ID** — for example `PID-0001`

## Which cities does ServeGo24 operate in?

ServeGo24 currently operates across **Hyderabad**, covering major areas and key localities including Madhapur, HITEC City, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Kukatpally, Jubilee Hills, Ameerpet and surrounding areas.
Our service coverage is continuously expanding to serve more customers across Hyderabad.

## What if no provider is available for the service I need?
If no eligible provider is available when you submit a booking, ServeGo24 automatically creates a No-Provider Request and sends it to the admin team.
The admin reviews the request in the **Admin Manual Booking Requests** tab, selects an eligible provider, and assigns the service request to them. Once assigned, the request becomes a confirmed booking, and the customer is notified.
