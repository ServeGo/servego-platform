# General FAQ

## Can you look up my booking, wallet or personal details?

No. This assistant answers general questions about how ServeGo24 works. It cannot see your
bookings, your wallet, your notifications or a provider's live location, and it cannot create,
change or cancel anything for you.

For anything about your own account, open your dashboard. If something is wrong, raise a
support ticket and the team will handle it.

## Why is my booking still Pending?

A Pending booking means your request has been sent out and nobody has accepted it yet. The
request is offered to every eligible provider for that service, and after 24 hours it is sent
out again rather than being closed — so it stays open until a provider takes it.

If no provider was eligible at all when you booked, ServeGo24 would have told you it sent a
No-Provider request to the admin team, who assign a provider manually.

## Why is no provider accepting my request?

A provider is only offered a request when they offer that service, their profile is verified,
their account is active, and their wallet is not negative. Requests go to all eligible
providers at once with no ranking, so a slow acceptance usually just means nobody has taken
it yet.

## Which cities is ServeGo24 available in?

Bookings are live in **Hyderabad** today. Bengaluru, Mumbai, Chennai and Delhi NCR are listed
as rollout cities, and coverage depends on which providers have set themselves up there.

## Do you operate 24/7?

ServeGo24 does not publish fixed support or service hours. Providers set their own
availability, so a late-night or early-morning job depends on whether a provider covering
your area is available at that time. Submit the booking and wait for a provider to accept.

For anything urgent, raise a support ticket.

## Is there a service fee for a customer?

No. ServeGo24 does not charge you the quotation amount — you pay the provider directly by cash
or UPI. The only ServeGo24 amount that can appear is a cancellation fee of **₹249** by default,
and only if you cancel after seeing a quotation. That record is display-only and is not a
real charge.

## How do I get paid as a provider?

The full quotation amount is recorded as your lifetime earnings. The platform commission
(10% by default) is debited from your wallet. Once your balance is positive you request a
withdrawal — minimum **₹100** by default — and the admin processes the payout to your bank.

## Why is my provider wallet negative?

Commission is debited from your wallet as bookings complete, and it can go negative before
enough earnings or credits clear it. While it is negative you stop receiving new requests
until it is cleared. Clear it by taking more bookings or paying the outstanding commission
once your balance allows a withdrawal.

## How do I become a provider?

Sign up as a Provider, complete your profile, apply for the services you want to offer, and
wait for the admin to verify your profile. See the Provider section for the full detail.
