# Disputes & Resolution

## What is a dispute?

A dispute is a disagreement between a customer and a provider about a booking. Common reasons:
- Provider did not arrive / significantly late
- Work quality not as expected
- Quotation amount disputed
- Provider cancelled after accepting
- Customer refused to pay after confirming quotation

## How do I raise a dispute?

**Submit a support ticket** from your dashboard:
1. Select the related booking
2. Choose subject (e.g., "Provider did not arrive", "Quotation dispute")
3. Describe the issue with details
4. Attach photos if relevant
5. Submit

The admin team reviews all tickets and responds with a resolution.

## What evidence helps resolve disputes?

- **Booking timeline** — auto-logged (accepted, on the way, arrived, quotation sent)
- **Live tracking map** — shows provider location history
- **Chat messages** — preset messages exchanged
- **Quotation** — itemised line items with amounts
- **Photos** — before/after work (attach to ticket)

## How does the admin resolve disputes?

The admin can:
- **Review full booking timeline** — every status change with timestamps
- **Check live tracking history** — provider location pings
- **Read chat logs** — all preset messages exchanged
- **View quotation** — line items and amounts
- **Contact both parties** for additional info

**Possible outcomes:**
- **Refund customer** (if provider at fault) — admin can adjust wallet
- **Pay provider** (if customer at fault) — admin can credit wallet
- **Cancel booking without fee** — remove cancellation fee
- **Mediate** — propose compromise
- **Escalate** — for serious issues

## What if the provider didn't arrive?

1. Check live tracking — provider may be on the way
2. Send preset message: "Are you on your way?"
3. If no response after reasonable time, raise support ticket
4. Admin can:
   - Cancel booking (no fee to customer)
   - Reassign to another provider
   - Mark provider cancellation on record

## What if the quotation seems too high?

1. **Do not confirm** — quotation is open for 24 hours
2. Raise support ticket with "Quotation dispute"
3. Admin reviews:
   - Line items vs. work described
   - Market rates for the service
   - Provider's explanation
4. Admin can:
   - Ask provider to revise quotation
   - Cancel booking without cancellation fee
   - Mediate a fair amount

## What if the customer refuses to pay after confirming?

- Quotation confirmation = agreement to pay that amount
- Provider can raise support ticket
- Admin reviews confirmation timestamp and quotation
- If customer confirmed, they are obligated to pay
- Admin can:
  - Record payment as completed in ledger
  - Credit provider's wallet if needed
  - Flag customer account for review

## Provider cancellation penalty

If a provider **accepts** a job then **cancels**:
- Cancellation recorded on provider's profile
- May affect provider rating/level progression
- If provider has active booking and cancels without valid reason, admin may:
  - Apply temporary account hold
  - Require explanation before reactivation

## Customer cancellation after quotation

If customer **confirms quotation** then disputes:
- Confirmation = binding agreement
- Admin will not refund unless provider failed to deliver
- Customer pays provider directly; platform only records ledger

## How long does resolution take?

- **Simple cases** (no-show, clear timeline): 24–48 hours
- **Complex cases** (quality disputes, evidence review): 3–5 business days
- **Urgent cases** (provider en route, safety): prioritized

## Can I appeal an admin decision?

Yes. Reply to the ticket with new evidence or clarification. The admin team re-reviews.
Final decisions are binding for the platform. Off-platform disputes (legal) are separate.
