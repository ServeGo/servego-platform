# Payments & Pricing

## How does payment work on ServeGo24?

ServeGo24 is not a payment gateway and does not move money. You pay the provider directly.

The flow is:

- The provider sends a quotation
- You approve the quotation
- You pay the provider directly, by cash or UPI, off the platform
- ServeGo24 records the amount as the provider's earnings
- The platform commission is debited from the **provider's** wallet

## Do I pay ServeGo24?

No. You never pay ServeGo24 the quotation amount. The only ServeGo24 amount that can appear
is a cancellation fee, and only if you cancel after seeing a quotation — see the Cancellation
& Support section.

## Is there a service fee when I confirm a quotation?

No. Confirming a quotation charges no fee at all. The quotation total is simply the
provider's price for the work, and a provider's quotation contains only their own payable
line items.

## Is the price on a service card final?

No. The price on a service card is a **starting price**. The final price is the provider's
quotation after they inspect the work, and you see it before anything is settled.

## What commission does ServeGo24 take?

The commission is **10% of the quotation total** by default. The admin can configure
different rates for different amount bands. It is always debited from the **provider's
wallet** — it is never charged to the customer.

## How do providers get paid out?

A provider requests a withdrawal of their positive wallet balance from their dashboard. The
admin approves or rejects it and transfers the money to the provider's bank.

- Minimum withdrawal is **₹100** by default
- There is **no maximum** by default
- Both limits are configurable by the admin

## How do I know the amount before I pay?

Always. The provider sends a quotation with itemised line items **after** the work is done,
and it stays open for review for 24 hours. You pay only if you approve the amount.

## Can I pay online through ServeGo24?

No. There is no online payment for a booking. All money moves directly between you and the
provider, and ServeGo24 only records it.
