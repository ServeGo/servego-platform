# Provider

## How do I become a ServeGo24 provider?

1. Sign up selecting the **Provider** role with your name, email and phone.
2. You are given a provider ID such as `PID-0001`, and a **wallet** is created for your
   earnings.
3. You start at **Bronze** level.
4. Complete your provider profile.
5. Apply for the services you want to offer.
6. Wait for the admin to review and verify your profile.

Once you are verified and offering a service, you start receiving requests for it.

## What do I need to fill in my profile?

- **Bio** — about yourself
- **Specialties** — what you are good at
- **Service areas** — the areas or localities you cover
- **Location** — your base location pinned on the map

## How do I apply to offer a service?

From your provider dashboard, apply for a specific service such as AC repair, giving:

- The service you are interested in
- Your years of experience
- A description of your experience with it, 10 to 1000 characters

The request goes to the ServeGo24 admin, and you can see whether it is Pending, Approved or
Denied.

## Do I need admin verification?

Yes. The admin verifies your profile. Until you are verified you do not receive requests,
regardless of how complete your profile is.

## Which requests will I receive?

You receive a request when:

- You have applied for that service and the admin has approved it
- Your profile is verified by the admin
- Your account is active
- Your wallet balance is not negative

There is no limit on how many open requests you can hold. You can accept or decline each request independently.

An open request (one you have not accepted yet) shows the **service** and the **city** only. The customer's full address, their instructions and their contact number are withheld until you **accept** the request. Once accepted, the Active Duty card reveals the complete address and phone so you can navigate and reach the customer.

## What do I do after I accept a job?

- The Active Duty card shows the live map for the whole trip until arrival.
- Mark **On the Way** when you leave.
- You are marked **Arrived** automatically when you come within about **150 metres** of the
  address; if GPS is unavailable you can mark it yourself.
- Do the work.
- Send the **quotation** with your line items for the customer to approve. It can be sent
  while the booking is Confirmed and stays open for 24 hours.
- The job completes when the customer confirms the quotation.

## How do I get paid?

ServeGo24 does not transfer money to you. After a booking completes, the full quotation
amount is recorded in your **earnings ledger** as lifetime earnings.

- The customer pays you **directly** — cash or UPI, off the platform.
- The platform **commission** (10% by default, and the admin can configure tiers) is debited
  from your **wallet**.
- Your wallet can go negative until you clear it, and while it is negative you **stop
  receiving new requests**.
- When your balance is positive you can request a **withdrawal**. The minimum is ₹100 by
  default and there is no maximum by default; the admin processes the payout to your bank.

## How do provider levels work?

Providers level up as they complete jobs:

- **Bronze** — starting level
- **Silver**, **Gold**, **Platinum**, **Diamond** — higher levels, unlocked as your monthly
  completed-job count grows

Levelling up credits a monthly incentive to your wallet, equal to a percentage of the
commission you paid. Your level does not change which requests you are offered.

## What other tools do providers get?

- **Availability schedule** — set the days and hours you are available
- **Online / offline toggle** — go offline when you are not working (this does not affect request eligibility; you still receive offers while offline)
- **Route plan** — an optimised route when you have several bookings in a day
- **Analytics** — your performance statistics
- **Badges** — earned for good performance
- **Invoices** — download the invoice for a completed booking
- **Support tickets** — raise issues with the platform
