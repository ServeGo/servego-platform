# Services

## What services can I book?

ServeGo24 is a home services marketplace. Customers can book services such as AC repair, plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, and other home services available in the ServeGo24 service catalog.

The service catalog is managed by the ServeGo24 admin team and can change over time.

The service catalog may include:

- Electrical work
- Plumbing
- AC servicing and repair
- Home cleaning
- Bathroom cleaning
- Washing machine repair
- Other services added by the ServeGo24 admin team

Customers can open the **Services** page to view the currently available services.

Each service can have a starting price. The starting price is not necessarily the final price.

## How do I book a service?

1. Open the **Services** page and select the service you need.
2. Click **Book Now** for the selected service.
3. Provide the required booking information, including the service location and other information requested by the booking form.
4. Submit the booking.

You receive a booking number such as `SG24-0001` after the booking is created.

The customer does **not** select a specific provider. ServeGo24 automatically searches for eligible providers for the requested service.

## What happens after I book a service?

After the booking is created, ServeGo24 searches for eligible providers.

Eligible providers receive a **lead notification** for the booking.

Providers can **Accept** or **Reject** the lead.

The first eligible provider to accept the lead gets the job.

After a provider accepts:

- The booking becomes confirmed.
- The provider is assigned to the booking.
- Other open offers for the same booking are automatically cancelled.
- The customer is notified with the provider's details.

## Can I choose which provider comes?

No. You do not select a provider yourself for a normal booking.

ServeGo24 sends the request to eligible providers for that service.

The first provider to accept the open offer gets the job.

Before accepting the lead, the provider receives limited information such as the service type and general area. The customer's full address and contact details are not provided at this stage.

After a provider accepts:

- The customer can see the provider's name and details.
- The provider can see the customer's full details required to perform the service.
- The customer can track the provider's live location while they travel to the service location.

## How does ServeGo24 find an eligible provider?

ServeGo24 checks providers who are eligible for the requested service.

A provider must generally have:

- An approved service for the requested category.
- An active and verified profile.
- A non-negative wallet balance.

Eligible providers receive the lead simultaneously.

Providers can **Accept** or **Reject** the offer.

If the first provider accepts:

- The booking becomes **CONFIRMED**.
- The provider is assigned to the booking.
- The lead becomes **ACCEPTED**.
- Other providers' open offers are automatically cancelled.

If a provider rejects the offer:

- Their offer is withdrawn.
- Other eligible providers can still accept the booking.
- If the last provider rejects the offer, the booking is cancelled.

## Why does a service show a starting price?

The price displayed on a service is a **starting price** and is not necessarily the final price.

After the provider assesses or performs the work, the provider sends a **quotation** containing itemised line items.

The customer reviews the quotation and confirms it.

The quotation amount represents the provider's price for the work.

## Can I book the same provider again?

There is no saved-provider list.

To request service again, create a new booking for the required service.

The new booking goes through the normal provider-matching process, and the request is sent to eligible providers for that service.

## What happens when the provider arrives?

After accepting the booking, the provider can see the customer's full address and travel to the service location.

The provider can mark **On the Way** when leaving.

The provider is marked **Arrived** automatically when their live location reaches approximately 150 metres from the customer's address.

If GPS is unavailable, the provider can use the manual **Arrive** option.

The customer receives notifications for these important booking stages.

## What is a quotation?

A **quotation** is the provider's proposed price for completing the work.

The quotation contains itemised line items.

The customer can review the quotation before confirming it.

Once the customer **confirms** the quotation, the booking is recorded as completed.

## What is a permanent, custom or no-provider request?

Beyond a normal booking there are three kinds of service request:

- **PERMANENT** — recurring or contract work such as monthly AC maintenance, weekly cleaning, or services involving a cook, maid or driver. The customer provides the service category, engagement type, start date, monthly budget, location and details. The admin team reviews the request and assigns a provider where appropriate.

- **CUSTOM** — a one-off job that is not available in the normal service catalog. The customer provides a custom service name and description. The admin team reviews the request and handles the provider assignment.

- **NO_PROVIDER** — a request used when no eligible provider is available for the requested service. The customer is informed and the request can be handled by the admin team. The admin reviews the request and can manually assign an appropriate provider.

The admin reviews these requests in the **Admin Permanent Services** tab and **Admin Manual Booking Requests** tab, approves or rejects them with a note where applicable, and assigns a provider where needed.

The customer is notified when the request status changes.

## What if no provider is available?

If no eligible provider is found for the requested service:

- The customer is informed that no provider is currently available.
- A **NO_PROVIDER** request can be handled through the admin team.
- The request enters the admin queue.
- The admin selects an appropriate provider from providers approved for that service.
- The admin assigns the provider.
- The booking can then be confirmed and the provider receives the job.

Common reasons a provider may not be available include:

- Their profile is incomplete or not verified.
- Their wallet balance is negative.
- They are not eligible for the requested service.
