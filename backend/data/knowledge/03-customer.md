# Customer

## How do I create a customer account?

You need your name, email address, phone number, a password and your home address, and you
pin your location on the map during sign-up.

Your address matters — it is what the provider travels to. Once you sign up you are given a
customer ID such as `CID-0001`.

## How do I manage my addresses?

You can save three addresses in your account — **Home**, **Office** and **Other** — and mark
one as your default. Saving them means you do not retype the location every time you book.

## Where do I see my bookings?

Your dashboard lists every booking you have made, current and past, with its status. Active
bookings also show the assigned provider and their live location on a map.

## How do I contact my provider during a booking?

Every active booking has a quick-messages panel with three preset messages:

- I'm at the location
- Please call me
- Are you on your way?

The panel is preset-only — there is no free-text message box.

## How do I leave a review?

After a booking is completed you can leave a star rating and a written review for the
provider. You can leave one review per completed booking, and it is published on the
provider's public profile.

## How do I contact support?

Raise a **support ticket** from your dashboard, with a subject and a description, linked to
the relevant booking if there is one. You do not need an account to reach us — the public
contact form on the website only needs your name, email, subject and message.

The ServeGo24 admin team reviews tickets and responds with a resolution.

## How do I reset my password?

Use **Forgot Password** on the login screen. ServeGo24 emails you a reset link; open it,
choose a new password, and sign in again.

## How do notifications work?

ServeGo24 sends real-time notifications for important events, and they appear without
refreshing the page:

- Booking submitted, accepted, cancelled or completed
- Provider on the way or arrived
- Quotation sent, approved or declined
- Withdrawal processed
- Permanent or contract request status changes

## Who can see my contact details?

Your **name** and **city** are visible to every provider who receives a broadcast offer for your request — that is so they can decide whether the job is worth travelling for.

Your **full address, any instructions you wrote, and your contact phone number** are only shared with the provider who actually **accepts** the request. Before that moment, no provider sees them.

If a provider accepts and the booking is later **cancelled**, your contact number is hidden again and cannot be re-shown.
